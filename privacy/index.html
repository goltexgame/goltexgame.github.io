Privacy Policy

GOLTEX – Online Disc Football · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Contact

For questions, bug reports and account requests: kurulbase@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Playing as a guest

You can play GOLTEX without an account. Guest progress is stored only on your device and no personal data is sent to the developer.

Data we collect when you sign in

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we store your account ID and email address (which may be an Apple private relay address), your club name, player ID, crest, stadium settings, game progress and friends list. This data is used only to save your progress, sync it across devices and let you play with friends.

Online matches

In an online match your club name, crest, cosmetics and in-match movements are shared with your opponent. Matches are not recorded.

Purchases

Gem packs and other in-app items are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment details; the developer never receives card or payment information.

Ads and tracking

We do not use third-party ad networks, analytics tools or advertising IDs, and we do not track you across apps or websites. Stadium billboards promote only the developer's own apps.

Data storage

Account data is stored on Supabase infrastructure over encrypted connections. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all server data: in the game go to Settings › Delete account, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes

Updates to this policy are posted on this page.
